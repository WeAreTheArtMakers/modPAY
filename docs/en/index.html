Keeping keys safe is not enough

Is securing digital assets only about storing private keys? Or is the real question how transactions are authorized, verified and recorded?

In early 2025, about 1.5 billion dollars were taken from a major exchange's cold wallet. No private key was stolen; the hardware wallets were in place. The signers approved a transaction that looked routine on screen, but the data they signed handed control of the wallet to the attacker. Every day, people lose assets the same way: by signing a transfer to a "poisoned" address that resembles one from their history, or an unlimited token approval.

That thought shaped modPAY. It rests on three principles:

  1. Your keys stay with you. Recovery phrases and keys never leave the device; the server and its administrators cannot reach them.
  2. You sign what you see. Signing Guard decodes the signed transaction on the device and compares network, recipient, amount, token, fee and signer with what was on screen. Any difference and nothing is sent. The wallet signs plain transfers only: no approvals, hidden contract calls or delegations.
  3. Nothing is booked until the chain confirms it. Payments and refunds reach the ledger only after on-chain confirmation.

Safe without being tiring: no extra step for verified merchants, your own accounts or addresses you paid before. A first-time recipient asks for the last 4 characters of the address; a lookalike of an address in your history asks for 8. Addresses are shown in easy-to-compare groups of four.

Features

Multi-network checkout

One invoice, several networks: the customer pays where they hold funds (e.g. USDC on Ethereum, Avalanche, Polygon or Arbitrum; USDT on BNB Smart Chain). The first payment closes the other options. No bridge, no FX risk.

BNB Smart Chain and Avalanche

Alongside Ethereum, Polygon and Arbitrum: BNB Smart Chain (BNB, USDT, USDC) and Avalanche C-Chain (AVAX, USDC). Fast blocks tracked, per-network confirmation depth.

KVKK / GDPR

Users erase their own account; personal data is anonymized, financial records stay for legal retention. Strictly necessary cookies only.

Signing Guard

The signed transaction is decoded on the device and compared with the screen; any difference and it is not sent. Warnings for address poisoning and hidden approvals.

Keys stay with the user

Recovery phrases and keys never leave the device; encrypted with Argon2id + AES-256-GCM. No administrator can access them.

QR payments

Enter an amount, get a QR. Customers pay in modPAY or any wallet such as MetaMask (EIP-681).

On-chain proof

A payment is confirmed only at the network's confirmation depth. Reorgs, partial and late payments get their own states.

Accounting-ready

Append-only double-entry ledger, reconciliation and trial balance. Every final payment posts automatically; exact-precision CSV exports for accounting.

Refunds

Signed by the merchant in their own wallet; booked once the exact transfer is verified on-chain.

Invoices paid from another network

Invoice on Avalanche, customer's USDC on Ethereum? They pay from there with Circle CCTP; modPAY completes the mint, so the customer needs no gas on the destination network.

Your brand, Turkish and English

Turkish and English interface. Your institution's name, colors and logo come from configuration, so the product runs under your brand. EURC for euro.

One-click demo

A new user creates a wallet and taps "Get test tokens" to receive test gas and USDC in seconds, then tries a payment or a cross-chain transfer right away. No faucet hunting.

Cross-chain USDC

With Circle CCTP, USDC is burned on one network and minted on another: no pool, no wrapped tokens, funds always in the user's own wallet. Every step (approval, burn, mint) is confirmed separately and Signing Guard checks each call byte for byte. On Ethereum, Avalanche, Arbitrum and Polygon test networks.

E-commerce API

Your shop backend opens invoices with an API key, tracks their status and sends the customer back to the shop after payment. Keys are limited to one workspace, stored hashed and revocable instantly.

Point of sale

The cashier enters an amount, one QR appears; the screen turns green when the payment is final. Runs on any tablet or phone, nothing to install.

Teams and integrations

Organizations, roles, email invitations, signed webhooks and two-factor authentication.

How it works

  1. The merchant creates a payment request; a unique amount and an expiry are assigned and a QR code is generated.
  2. The customer scans it, sees network, recipient, amount and maximum fee, and confirms. The transaction is signed on their device.
  3. The worker finds the transfer on-chain and counts confirmations; payments from external wallets are detected without a hash.
  4. At the required depth the request is confirmed; ledger posting, event and webhook are produced in one database transaction.

Security

Encrypted on the device

Argon2id + AES-256-GCM; auto-lock; password again for every export.

Explicit consent

No transaction is signed before the user reviews and confirms the values.

Hardened sessions

HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Strict cookies; CSRF; lockout; rate limits; TOTP.

Immutable records

Ledger and audit rows are protected by database triggers.

Strict CSP

Same-origin only; no inline scripts or styles.

Kubernetes

Non-root, read-only containers; NetworkPolicies; restricted Pod Security.

Compliance: controls mapped to SOC 2 and ISO/IEC 27001:2022. modPAY does not store, process or transmit card data, so it is outside PCI DSS scope. KVKK/GDPR: privacy notice, self-service erasure and retention in the product. The security review report and control matrix are shared during licensing talks.
