modPAY is a non-custodial digital wallet, QR token-payment infrastructure and payment-operations platform for business. Payments are signed in the browser, verified on-chain and posted to an append-only ledger in SQL Server.

Currently runs on test networks only and has not had an independent security audit. modPAY is not a bank, payment institution or custodian.

Features

Keys stay with the user

Recovery phrases and keys never leave the device; encrypted with Argon2id + AES-256-GCM. No administrator can access them.

QR payments

Enter an amount, get a QR. Customers pay in modPAY or any wallet such as MetaMask (EIP-681).

On-chain proof

A payment is confirmed only at the network's confirmation depth. Reorgs, partial and late payments get their own states.

Accounting-ready

Append-only double-entry ledger, reconciliation and trial balance. Every final payment posts automatically; exact-precision CSV exports for accounting.

Refunds

Signed by the merchant in their own wallet; booked once the exact transfer is verified on-chain.

Teams and integrations

Organizations, roles, email invitations, signed webhooks and two-factor authentication.

How it works

  1. The merchant creates a payment request; a unique amount and an expiry are assigned and a QR code is generated.
  2. The customer scans it, sees network, recipient, amount and maximum fee, and confirms. The transaction is signed on their device.
  3. The worker finds the transfer on-chain and counts confirmations; payments from external wallets are detected without a hash.
  4. At the required depth the request is confirmed; ledger posting, event and webhook are produced in one database transaction.

Security

Encrypted on the device

Argon2id + AES-256-GCM; auto-lock; password again for every export.

Explicit consent

No transaction is signed before the user reviews and confirms the values.

Hardened sessions

HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Strict cookies; CSRF; lockout; rate limits; TOTP.

Immutable records

Ledger and audit rows are protected by database triggers.

Strict CSP

Same-origin only; no inline scripts or styles.

Kubernetes

Non-root, read-only containers; NetworkPolicies; restricted Pod Security.

No independent security audit yet; no PCI DSS, SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certification. Mainnet broadcasting is disabled.
